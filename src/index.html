Welcome to The 6th ICLLLE 2026!

We are proud to present this international conference, organized by the Faculty of Arts and Education, Universitas Teknokrat Indonesia. This year marks the sixth ICLLLE, following the success of the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 5th conferences. With the theme “Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies: Transforming Language, Literature, Linguistics, and Education for the Future,” the conference explores how emerging technologies are reshaping language, literary studies, linguistics, and education, from artificial intelligence in language to digital literacy and contemporary digital culture. Our goal is to facilitate productive academic dialogue, enrich teaching methodologies, and provide publication opportunities for researchers. Held on November 26, 2026 through ZOOM and hosted in collaboration not only with regional partners but also with international institutions, ICLLLE 2026 warmly welcomes participants from across the globe. We wish you insightful discussions, fruitful collaborations, and inspiring explorations into the future of the social sciences and humanities!
